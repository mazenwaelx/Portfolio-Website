## Filters to apply on every alert
- Experience: **Entry level / Associate / Trainee**
- Date posted: **Past 24 hours** (catches the freshest posts)
- Sort: **Most recent**

## Weekly cadence
- **Mon:** skim new alerts, add any strong fits to `apply-tracker.csv`
- **Tue–Thu:** apply to 3–5 per day (priority 1 first)
- **Fri:** follow-ups on anything 5+ days silent
